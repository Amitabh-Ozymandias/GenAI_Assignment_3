Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between TechCorp Inc. ("Provider") and ClientCo LLC ("Client") as of January 1, 2025.

1. Scope of Services
The Provider agrees to deliver software development, maintenance, and consulting services as described in individual Statements of Work (SOWs) executed under this Agreement.

2. Term and Termination
This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years from the Effective Date. Either party may terminate this Agreement with 90 days written notice.

3. Payment Terms
Client shall pay Provider within 30 days of receipt of invoice. Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month.

4. Confidentiality
Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary information disclosed during the term of this Agreement. This obligation survives termination.

5. Intellectual Property
All work product created under this Agreement shall be owned by Client upon full payment. Provider retains rights to pre-existing tools and methodologies.

6. Limitation of Liability
Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Provider's total liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the 12 months preceding the claim.

7. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
